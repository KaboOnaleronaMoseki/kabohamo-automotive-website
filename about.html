OUR STORY

Making Vehicle Administration More Convenient

Kabohamo Automotive presents itself as a business focused on helping customers with vehicle-related administrative processes, including licensing, registration and number plates.

Providing accessible, transparent, and prompt automotive support for individual vehicle owners and commercial fleet managers across South Africa.

To provide convenient, professional assistance that simplifies vehicle-related processes and saves client time.

To become a trusted, premier provider of accessible vehicle administration and logistics support services.
